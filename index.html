YOUR TOOLKIT

Keep your focus.
Keep your controls.

Eight optional components. Existing replay and audio libraries carry forward under the Xavier Bot name.

01 / REPLAY + ANALYSIS

Test the timing.
Keep the evidence.

FramePilot tests shifted inputs against the game’s physics. Saved analysis avoids doing the same work again, while unresolved inputs stay unmeasured. Dual-input mirroring records both player tracks. Lossless AC compression stores repeated input sequences compactly.

1f2f3f4f5–6f7–8f

Compression preserves input order and ticks; the two-million expanded-input limit still applies. Analysis speed depends on the level, macro and computer.

02 / AUDIO

Your soundtrack.
Your click sound.

Jukebox keeps selected NONGs and offsets ready. Clicks adds 11 bundled community packs with 295 samples alongside the Kenney defaults. Choose separate P1/P2 packs, volume and pitch variation.

03 / PRACTICE

Know your run.

Run Info saves attempts and completions. Utilities adds noclip with a hit limit, tint and collision-free simulation-time accuracy.

Tagged noclip runs stay out of regular totals. Optional recording recovery returns after a confirmed death by replaying and verifying the saved prefix in a full classic level.

04 / SHOWCASE

Keep the footage.

Replay video capture and optional Death Replay clips can include game audio. Choose the settings that fit your machine.

Death Replay’s rolling buffer starts off; enable it in the menu.

05 / INPUT

Between the frames.

Optional CBF adds timestamped input substeps and suspends while FramePilot owns macro input. Input HUD makes your presses visible.

Use one CBF implementation at a time.
